# The First Week With Smart Glasses: Check Ordinary Use Before the Return Window

A [Ray-Ban Meta owner report](https://www.reddit.com/r/RaybanMeta/comments/1x3o570/meta_not_helping_with_new_glasses_that_arrived/) describes prescription glasses arriving with a lens scuff and delays obtaining a return label. Another participant reports a faster process. These are individual accounts, not a verified service-time average or a finding about responsibility. The original post does not establish a model generation, final resolution or terms that apply to your purchase.

A first-week checklist should preserve evidence and check the intended routine, rather than subjecting a new device to stress tests. Start with the seller's actual return and service terms; do not invent a universal deadline from another buyer's experience.

## Record the condition as received

Inspect the glasses and supplied accessories in normal light before extended use. If there is an apparent scuff or missing item, document it privately with the packaging and order details, then contact the seller through its normal process. Do not polish, disassemble or modify a disputed defect while waiting for instructions.

Keep a record of the support case and promised next step. If a return label has not arrived, follow up through the same legitimate channel rather than posting order numbers publicly. Preserve proof of any handover or shipment you later make.

## Check the activities you actually bought

Complete normal setup and supported updates, then try a short ordinary session: fit, audio, a consented call if relevant, a capture, import and playback. Check reconnection after normal storage. Confirm the specific assistant or translation function you need on the actual phone and account, rather than relying on a demonstration from another model.

[Ray-Ban's official Meta FAQ](https://www.ray-ban.com/usa/c/frequently-asked-questions-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses) supplies manufacturer setup and care guidance. Do not test water resistance, force repeated full battery depletion or disable capture indicators to complete a checklist. Discomfort is a reason to stop and reassess fit, not a test to endure.

## Preserve the files before resolving the hardware

Import recordings you intend to keep and confirm that they open outside the glasses before a factory reset or return. Follow the supported account-removal process when appropriate, while retaining the purchase and case records you need. Never send passwords or authenticator codes with a support report.

The result can be simple: supported tasks work, a specific limitation is unacceptable, or a problem needs assessment. None requires declaring an entire product defective from one experience.
