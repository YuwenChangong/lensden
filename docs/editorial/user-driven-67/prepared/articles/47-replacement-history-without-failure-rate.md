# A Second Pair Has a Different Problem: Keep the Replacement History Separate

In a [UK One Pro returns account](https://www.reddit.com/r/Xreal/comments/1wdhebj/uk_returns_turnaround/), an owner reports a dark pixel, sends the pair back and later updates the thread after receiving a replacement with a different visual complaint. The update does not establish the outcome of a third pair. It should not be summarised as either a successful final fix or a product-wide failure rate.

The useful lesson is how to preserve an exchange history without combining every symptom into one diagnosis. Different units can produce different observations, and a replacement process can have several dates that are easy to confuse.

## Give each unit its own record

Keep the order and support reference, the unit identity privately where needed, the observed symptom and the date it was reported. Separate a dark pixel from coloured ghosting or a soft image. Do not assume the same cause because the owner encountered both during one purchasing experience.

Use harmless sample content when documenting a display issue. A through-lens photograph can help illustrate the complaint, but note how it was captured and what it fails to reproduce. Do not publish serial numbers, shipping labels or support correspondence containing personal information as part of a public review.

## Distinguish the shipping milestones

Returned to the carrier, delivered to the warehouse, inspected, replacement registered and replacement received are different events. Preserve actual notifications instead of treating a warehouse-delivery scan as proof that inspection is complete. The anecdote's turnaround is one case, not a service commitment for a different seller or country.

Consult the manufacturer's own regional store and the actual seller's written policy. [XREAL's official store](https://us.shop.xreal.com/) supplies a starting point for manufacturer contacts, but its US terms should not be presented as the rules for a UK purchase. Ask the relevant seller how the open case and return deadline will be handled.

## Decide whether another exchange answers the problem

An exchange can be reasonable when support identifies a suitable next step. It is also reasonable to ask what evidence would distinguish another unit from a persistent fit mismatch. Preserve the option to stop the purchasing experiment rather than regarding repeated exchanges as mandatory.
