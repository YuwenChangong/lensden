# When Glasses Captions Fall Behind, Check the Display and the Conversation

Subtitles are useful only if you can connect them to the right spoken turn. An [IT Home hands-on account of RayNeo iO](https://www.ithome.com/0/993/065.htm) describes English audio translation into displayed subtitles during an office scenario and reports a short delay. That is one reviewer's observation on a display-equipped model, not a Lensden timing benchmark or a guarantee for other languages and environments.

RayNeo iO is also different from V3 camera glasses or ordinary audio-and-camera Ray-Ban Meta models. A translation feature name does not establish that a product contains a display. Check the hardware before deciding how captions would fit your routine.

## Identify what is arriving late

Distinguish speech recognition, translation and displaying the result. Captions in the original language and translated subtitles add different processing requirements. A late phrase can be accurate in isolation while appearing to answer the wrong speaker.

In an informal, consented exchange, try short turns and let the caption finish before responding. For a prerecorded video, pausing or replaying a phrase offers a recovery option that live conversation does not. Do not assume a successful quiet office demonstration covers overlapping speakers at a crowded event.

## Check the supported language and presentation

Confirm the input and output languages, translation direction and exact mode in the model's documentation and current app. [RayNeo's manufacturer support hub](https://www.rayneo.cn/help.html) is a starting point, but an older Air FAQ is not an iO caption specification. Leave undocumented pair support and offline availability Unknown.

Display placement matters as well as response time. Can you glance at the line, return attention to the speaker and understand who said what? Those are personal usability questions, not reasons to invent a universal acceptable delay. If reading the display distracts from the situation, stop and use another communication method.

## Preserve details that must be exact

Ask for names, addresses, amounts and instructions in a form you can check. Glasses captions should not be the sole channel for consequential medical, legal or emergency information. Misheard input can produce a plausible translation, so a clear-looking subtitle is not independent confirmation.

When reporting a problem, include the model, software version if available, language pair, mode and whether the source was live speech or a recording. Keep the other person's identity and private conversation out of public examples.
