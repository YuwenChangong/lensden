# The Insert Is Part of the Price: Budgeting for Prescription Display Glasses

In an [XREAL prescription discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Xreal/comments/1tlksej/), prospective buyers ask whether the displays will be clear without their ordinary glasses, while existing wearers describe different insert purchases and results. A price remembered by one commenter is not a current quote, and a satisfactory movie experience is not proof that another person can read small desktop text without correction.

The useful purchasing exercise is to price a complete, model-specific arrangement before treating the glasses' shelf price as the final budget. Prescription requirements are personal; Lensden cannot determine them from a forum post.

## Ask for a complete quote

Identify the exact frame edition and the insert mounting system it uses. Ask the optical supplier whether the quote includes the carrier, fitted lenses, any chosen coating, shipping and local taxes. Clarify whether an eye examination or additional fitting measurements are separate. Do not assume an empty insert carrier supplied with the glasses includes finished prescription lenses.

Keep optional upgrades separate from requirements. A coating selected by one user may change their order total without being necessary for yours. Ask the supplier to explain alternatives using the prescription and the intended frame, rather than copying a basket from an online recommendation.

## Check the cost of changing your mind

The retailer's glasses return policy and the optical supplier's custom-lens policy may differ. Obtain both in writing before ordering. If the frame is returned, find out whether the insert can be reused on the exact replacement model; do not assume that a common brand name makes mounts interchangeable.

The [manufacturer's One-series guide](https://us.shop.xreal.com/blogs/buying-guide/user-guide_xreal-one-series) distinguishes prescription frames for different product families. It does not establish a universal third-party lens price or return entitlement. The source thread also spans different models, so its totals should not be treated as comparable quotations.

## Compare the completed setups

Put the ordinary glasses purchase, the prescription arrangement and any necessary connection accessory on separate lines. Record the currency and quote date. If a cost is unresolved, retain an Unknown entry instead of assigning zero. This makes it possible to compare a display-glasses arrangement with a portable monitor without hiding the accessory budget.

Before ordering, confirm that the optical provider accepts the full prescription information needed for that design. A diopter figure in a product listing is not the same thing as a completed prescription consultation.
