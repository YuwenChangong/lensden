# Air 4 Pro's Bottom Edge Is Hard to See: Do Not Chase a Better Picture by Forcing the Frame

In a [model-specific Air 4 Pro review](https://www.androidauthority.com/rayneo-air-4-pro-review-3659545/), Android Authority's tester describes fit affecting edge visibility and desktop interface elements. This is an observation by that reviewer, not evidence that most buyers will need modifications or that every unit has the same optical result.

It offers a useful distinction from a missing video signal. If the top of the picture is clear but the bottom menu is difficult to see, replacing an HDMI cable may not address the wearer's viewing position. First establish what is actually visible in a straightforward supported connection.

## Use the lower interface deliberately

Choose a harmless page with labels at the bottom and corners, not just a film whose action stays near the centre. Check whether the lower label is absent, visible but soft, or visible only while holding the frame. Keep the source and output mode unchanged while observing.

Try only the fit adjustments documented for the exact model. A smaller advertised image on a different product does not establish that Air 4 Pro provides the same control. Do not force the nose-pad bracket or widen the frame beyond the maker's instructions to imitate an online modification.

## Include your prescription arrangement

If an insert or ordinary glasses are involved, disclose that to the optical provider or seller. Additional spacing can change the arrangement being evaluated. An aftermarket nose pad that fits the bare frame may still interfere with an insert; the accessory supplier should confirm the exact combination.

[RayNeo's Air 4 Pro page](https://www.rayneo.com/products/rayneo-air-4-pro-ar-smart-glasses) identifies the product and its intended source arrangements. The reviewer and the product page do not certify an unapproved pad, a home-made support or a fit for an individual wearer.

## Preserve a sensible purchase decision

If every supported comfortable arrangement leaves essential controls unusable, record that outcome and ask about the return process. A device can produce an attractive movie image while failing a particular desktop task. That is a meaningful suitability decision without establishing a manufacturing defect.

Keep the supplied parts and avoid permanent alterations during the trial. A fix that requires pressing the frame into the face is not a sustainable demonstration of normal use. [Lensden's display guide](/guides/xr-display-specifications/) explains why display resolution alone cannot settle this requirement.
