# Prescription Inserts Are Not Interchangeable Just Because the Prescription Is

An owner in [this insert-compatibility discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Xreal/comments/1vah54s/) asks whether a One Pro insert can move to an XBX model. Replies describe incompatible parts and improvised adapters. That is a useful warning about asking the mounting question, not evidence that cutting or gluing an insert is an appropriate solution.

For a wearer with astigmatism, the problem is larger than whether a carrier clicks into place. The [National Eye Institute](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/astigmatism) explains that astigmatism can cause blurred or distorted vision and that correction is a matter for appropriate optical care. A software clarity setting cannot establish whether an individual's prescription is correct.

## Keep three compatibility questions separate

First, does the carrier mechanically belong to this exact glasses model? Second, can the optical supplier make and fit lenses for the wearer's complete prescription in that carrier? Third, is the assembled arrangement comfortable and usable with the display? A yes to the first question does not answer the next two.

Provide the optical supplier with the exact product and carrier identity. Do not reduce a prescription to a single short-sightedness value because a product advertises a diopter adjustment. Ask the supplier what information they need; do not infer cylinder, axis or fitting measurements from a social-media photograph.

## Why a home-made mount is not a validated shortcut

The thread contains a modification involving a different carrier. Lensden has not evaluated its alignment, durability or interaction with the display. A part that can be attached is not automatically correctly positioned for a particular prescription. Changing the lens shape or mounting angle also goes beyond the simple question of reusing a purchase.

[XREAL's model-specific prescription guidance](https://tutorials.xreal.com/docs/glasses/xbxa01/first-use/prescription-lens/) is the manufacturer reference to consult, together with the optical provider. If the supplier cannot confirm the proposed combination, retain compatibility as Unknown rather than letting a forum workaround fill the gap.

## Preserve a practical return route

Keep the original carrier and packaging intact while checking fit. Ask separately about the glasses' return terms and the custom lenses' terms. If an arrangement remains unclear or uncomfortable, stop using it and obtain appropriate advice instead of trying to train through the problem.
