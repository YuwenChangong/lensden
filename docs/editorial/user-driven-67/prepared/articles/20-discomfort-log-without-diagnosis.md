# A Headache After AR Glasses Is Not an Adaptation Schedule

A [One Pro owner using XREAL Eye](https://www.reddit.com/r/Xreal/comments/1vf896h/) describes headaches that continued after the viewing session and asks whether everyone eventually adapts. Other participants report different experiences. The thread cannot establish a normal adaptation period, a medical cause or how frequently the problem occurs.

The appropriate first decision is whether to stop the session, not which speculative diagnosis to choose. Persistent pain or a vision change belongs with an appropriate healthcare professional. Lensden has not examined the wearer or tested the hardware, and does not recommend continuing use to satisfy a fixed number of days.

## Describe what happened without naming a cause

If preparing a support report, distinguish a sore contact point from difficulty viewing the image. Record where the frame touched, the approximate duration before discomfort and whether symptoms continued after removal. Avoid reproducing a painful session merely to obtain a more exact timing.

Include the device edition, prescription arrangement if relevant, source and selected display mode. A note about prescription use does not require publishing a full medical prescription on a forum. Share only information actually needed by the appropriate support or care provider.

## Do not convert comments into medical findings

The cited discussion proposes explanations involving fit, display behaviour and individual tolerance. Those proposals are not evidence that one component caused the symptoms. One participant's successful setting does not establish a safe treatment for someone else. Equally, a symptom reported with Eye attached does not establish that positional tracking caused it.

The [National Eye Institute's refractive-error information](https://medlineplus.gov/refractiveerrors.html) provides a general distinction between vision problems and their clinical assessment. For intended mechanical adjustments, use the [manufacturer's One-series guide](https://us.shop.xreal.com/blogs/buying-guide/user-guide_xreal-one-series). Neither source diagnoses this particular report.

## Keep the buying decision separate

Ask the seller about returning a frame that cannot be used comfortably under its documented conditions. Record the deadline while seeking advice; do not let an open-ended online experiment replace that conversation. If a device does not suit the wearer, that does not require proving that all units are faulty.

For future trials, judge the complete intended task and retain a conventional display option. [Lensden's work-monitor guide](/guides/ar-glasses-coding-virtual-monitors/) discusses workflow limits without promising all-day comfort.
