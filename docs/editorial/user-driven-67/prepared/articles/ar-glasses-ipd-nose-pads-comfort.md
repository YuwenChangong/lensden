# The Fit Test Before Buying AR Glasses: IPD, Nose Pads and Screen Edges

A number on a specification sheet can tell you the field of view. It cannot tell you whether both of your eyes will see the whole picture when a frame rests on your nose. Owners in an [XREAL One Pro discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Xreal/comments/1lqnvkj/xreal_one_pro_blur_issue_around_edges/) described blurred edges despite trying software and nose-pad adjustments. In a separate [RayNeo Air 4 Pro account](https://www.reddit.com/r/RayNeo/comments/1tr4jsb/rayneo_air_4_pro_first_impressions_heating/), a buyer said the bottom of the image was difficult to see because of the fit. These are individual experiences, not a controlled comparison.

## Measure before you buy

Interpupillary distance, or IPD, describes the spacing between your pupils. Product ranges are model-specific, and a broad listed range is not a promise of uniform clarity at every value. Ask an optician for a measurement if you are unsure. Check whether a model comes in more than one optical size, and whether the retailer accepts returns after a careful indoor fitting test.

## A ten-minute fitting routine

Start with the default nose pads. Centre a test page showing text in every corner; then change one adjustment at a time. Try reading the menu bar, not just watching a trailer. Can you see the lower line without pushing the glasses into your face? Is the image equally comfortable through each eye? Does the nose pad stay in place after normal head movement? Take note of pressure around the temples after several minutes.

## Why a huge virtual screen can make things worse

Enlarging the displayed workspace may push interface elements into the less comfortable edges of the optical window. A smaller screen can be more useful for work even if it looks less impressive in a promotional demo. The intended use matters: dialogue subtitles and console menus impose different demands from landscape video.

## What a trial cannot prove

Ten minutes will not predict a three-hour session perfectly, but it can expose immediate mismatches. If a frame gives you persistent discomfort, nausea or visual strain, take it off rather than forcing adaptation. And avoid modifying or stretching hinges beyond the documented adjustments.

## Check the exact frame edition

The [manufacturer's One-series guide](https://us.shop.xreal.com/blogs/buying-guide/user-guide_xreal-one-series) documents supplied nose-pad choices and model-specific prescription inserts. One Pro M and L are distinct fitting choices, not software upgrades. The cited owners' firmware versions are not established, and their different faces prevent a controlled optical comparison. Treat physical fit as a purchase requirement rather than a defect diagnosis.
