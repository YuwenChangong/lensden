# When Glasses Slide Down: The Trade-off Between a Clear Picture and a Stable Fit

An [XREAL One Pro nose-slip discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Xreal/comments/1qyq629/xreal_one_pro_nose_slip/) raises a problem specifications rarely capture: an initially good image may not stay in place during ordinary movement. The thread offers accessory suggestions, but those suggestions are not proof that an unapproved pad or support will fit every wearer safely.

For a buying decision, distinguish a frame that is uncomfortable from one that cannot maintain its viewing position. A softer contact surface may feel better initially while allowing the frame to move. A tighter arrangement may hold the image but introduce pressure. Neither trade-off can be resolved from a photograph of someone else's face.

## Observe one ordinary session

Start with the supplied arrangement and the manufacturer's documented adjustments. Read a page that uses the upper and lower edges, then perform normal seated movements. Note whether the frame settles, whether text disappears and whether you repeatedly hold the glasses in place. Do not test by deliberately shaking the frame or putting strain on its cable.

Change one supported fit option and repeat. Record the nose-pad choice and temple position with the observation. A short note such as “lower menu disappears after looking down” is more actionable than a general rating for comfort. Stop if the arrangement causes persistent discomfort rather than tightening it further.

## Treat aftermarket fixes as a separate purchase

Ask the accessory supplier whether the part is designed for the exact model and whether it interferes with prescription inserts or the frame's intended adjustment. A forum suggestion can generate this question; it cannot provide the supplier's answer. Avoid adhesive, cutting or stretching changes that might damage optics or complicate a return.

[XREAL's One-series guide](https://us.shop.xreal.com/blogs/buying-guide/user-guide_xreal-one-series) is the primary reference for its supplied fit options. Recommendations intended for an older Air frame should not automatically be carried over to One Pro. The discussion does not establish a single firmware version, and a software update cannot itself document the mechanical fit of a third-party part.

## Decide whether the arrangement is sustainable

For occasional viewing, a small adjustment at the beginning may be acceptable. For work requiring stable edge visibility, frequent repositioning may defeat the intended use. Make that judgement during the return window, while retaining the original parts.
