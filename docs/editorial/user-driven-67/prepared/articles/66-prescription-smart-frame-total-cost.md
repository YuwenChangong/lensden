# Price the Prescription Smart Frame, Not Just the Advertised Glasses

A [Ray-Ban Meta buyer question](https://www.reddit.com/r/RaybanMeta/comments/1nbsi87/can_i_buy_meta_glasses_and_add_prescription/) asks whether prescription lenses can be added after buying the glasses. Replies suggest different vendors and approaches, but do not establish one supported process or current price for every frame and country. The exact generation and the buyer's eventual result are not confirmed.

The useful purchasing question is the complete quote for your intended configuration, including what happens when the prescription changes. Camera glasses that also serve as everyday eyewear have different costs from a removable insert in wired display glasses.

## Ask for the finished configuration

Get a quote for the named frame, size, lens prescription, selected lens options and fitting service. Ask which organisation is responsible for the electronics and which supplies the lenses. Keep required items separate from optional tint, coatings or accessories so that comparisons use the same configuration.

[Ray-Ban's official Meta FAQ](https://www.ray-ban.com/usa/c/frequently-asked-questions-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses) is the manufacturer reference for supported products and prescription guidance. Confirm eligibility with the relevant authorised seller or optical provider. Do not assume that a forum account of a lens replacement means every optical shop supports every smart frame.

## Include the next lens change

Ask how a future prescription update is handled, whether the existing frame can be reused through a supported service and what assessment is required. Request current charges and the effect on the applicable warranty and service arrangements. A historical comment or promotion is not a present quote.

Check cancellation, fitting and return terms before ordering customised lenses, especially where those terms differ from standard stock glasses. Lensden is not determining legal rights or endorsing a particular retailer. Keep order documents and health-related prescription details private.

## Do not make DIY the hidden budget assumption

Popping lenses out based on a comment may risk damage or an unsupported configuration. Use the provider's supported process rather than treating an improvised replacement as a guaranteed saving. Similarly, do not assume a different generation's case or charging accessory is compatible because the frame looks similar.

For software features, distinguish the normal companion app from optional third-party services. Do not add an unverified subscription to the comparison, or promise that every future assistant capability is included. Record unknown recurring costs as Unknown.
