# Ordinary Glasses Under Display Glasses: A Temporary Fit Is Not a Compatibility Guarantee

A [discussion about alternatives to prescription inserts](https://www.reddit.com/r/Xreal/comments/1qfiapp/) includes a commenter who says two ordinary frames fit under Air 2 Pro. The original poster, discussing a different fitting problem, later reports visiting an optometrist and ordering inserts. Those experiences should not be collapsed into one recommendation for every face or product.

The question is not simply whether two frames can be worn at the same time. They must avoid damaging each other, maintain a usable position and allow appropriate correction. An arrangement that produces an image for a minute may still be inconvenient or uncomfortable for the task the buyer intends.

## Ask about the physical arrangement

Check the exact glasses model and ordinary-frame dimensions with the optical provider or retailer. Do not press lenses together or force the display frame outward to copy an online photograph. A narrow frame used by one commenter is not evidence that a thicker prescription lens or a different temple shape will fit.

Describe the intended use: a quick demonstration, a film or sustained reading. Keep the question about a trial separate from whether the manufacturer recommends the arrangement for regular use. The [One-series guide](https://us.shop.xreal.com/blogs/buying-guide/user-guide_xreal-one-series) documents dedicated, model-specific prescription options; a guide for one family should not be treated as an Air 2 Pro fitting certificate.

## Check what the extra spacing changes

If a documented temporary trial is appropriate, note whether the whole image remains visible and whether either frame moves when looking down. An additional frame can change the resting position, so the result is not necessarily comparable with a store demo performed without it. Stop if either frame contacts the optics or causes discomfort.

Keep supplied parts and protective packaging intact while deciding. Avoid permanently altering a nose pad, hinge or prescription carrier before understanding both return policies. If an optical supplier proposes a different arrangement, ask which exact frame and mounting system it covers.

## Do not use a fit anecdote as an eye prescription

Someone else's ability to read the image without inserts does not establish your own correction needs. Questions about astigmatism, prism or other individual requirements should be directed to the optical provider, with the complete prescription where necessary.
