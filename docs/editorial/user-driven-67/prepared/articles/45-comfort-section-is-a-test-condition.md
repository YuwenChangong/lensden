# Why a Positive Air 4 Pro Review Still Needs a Comfort Section

An [Air 4 Pro discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/RayNeo/comments/1s590v5/my_experience_so_far_with_the_air_4_pro/) includes wearers who enjoy the image yet remain distracted by nose pressure or precise positioning. That combination is not contradictory. A device can be enjoyable for one task while its physical arrangement limits another.

Comfort should therefore be reported as a condition of the experience, not a one-word verdict appended to the specifications. A reviewer saying a frame felt good does not establish how it will sit with another prescription insert, head shape or pair of headphones. It also does not establish how long that person used it.

## Ask what the review actually exercised

Was the writer seated, reclining or repeatedly looking between a physical keyboard and the display? Did the session use games, subtitles or small desktop text? A short entertainment demonstration may never require access to a menu near the optical edge. The conditions explain why two honest reviews can differ.

Keep any aftermarket modification visible in the account. A result obtained after replacing parts is not an out-of-box result. Lensden is not recommending the source discussion's improvised lens or frame alterations, and has not evaluated their safety or alignment.

## Use supported adjustments as the baseline

[RayNeo's model page](https://www.rayneo.com/products/rayneo-air-4-pro-ar-smart-glasses) and the supplied manual are the appropriate starting references for Air 4 Pro. Ask the seller which fit changes are intended and whether a prescription insert changes the arrangement. Do not assume instructions for another RayNeo generation apply.

During a permitted ordinary trial, record whether the frame remains in place and whether the required interface is usable without holding it. If discomfort develops, stop rather than completing an arbitrary endurance target. A product review is not a medical adaptation schedule.

## Turn the experience into a buying requirement

For a buyer who needs an occasional film screen, modest repositioning may be acceptable. For work, repeated interruption can matter more than an impressive display specification. Decide which requirement is essential before comparing scores from different reviewers.
