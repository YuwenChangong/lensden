# A Translation Language List Is Not an Offline Download List

Before travelling, check whether your exact language pair is downloadable on your exact glasses and app. A [Ray-Ban Meta community discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/RaybanMeta/comments/1tqzfnc/translation_language_pack_update/) includes a Blayzer owner's report of a limited pack selection and discussion of software versions and rollout differences. It does not establish one current worldwide pack count for every Meta glasses model.

The important distinction is between a language supported somewhere in the service and one available without a network in the mode you intend to use. An online feature list cannot settle the second question.

## Check both ends of the conversation

Identify the spoken language, the output language, whether translation is one-way or two-way, and whether output is audio or text. Then check the actual app's download controls and the product's documentation. Do not assume that installing one pack automatically enables every pair containing that language.

[Meta's June 2026 announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/meta-essilorluxottica-partner-launch-meta-glasses/) describes expanded language support, while [Ray-Ban's product FAQ](https://www.ray-ban.com/usa/c/frequently-asked-questions-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses) provides manufacturer guidance for the named products. Neither should be silently converted into a guarantee that an absent download option exists on your account.

## Finish setup before the connection disappears

Where downloads are supported, complete them over a normal connection before departure and confirm that the app reports completion. In an ordinary, safe setting, check the intended supported offline mode with a simple consented exchange. Keep the phone available; loss of internet and loss of the glasses-to-phone connection are different conditions.

An error during a practice attempt is useful evidence to resolve before travelling. Save the model, app and firmware versions and the language pair. Do not repeatedly reinstall, reset or change account regions just to chase an option shown in somebody else's screenshot. Those changes can create new problems without proving eligibility.

## Preserve an honest fallback

Keep important names, destinations and bookings accessible in another form. If the language cannot be selected or its offline status is undocumented, mark it Unknown and plan around that limitation. Avoid treating a hoped-for update as a travel requirement already met.

This check concerns availability, not a measured translation-quality comparison. A pack that installs successfully can still have limitations in noise, timing or vocabulary.
