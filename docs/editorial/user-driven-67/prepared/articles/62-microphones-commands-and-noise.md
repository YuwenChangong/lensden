# Voice Commands Fail for Different Reasons: Separate the Microphone From the Reply

A glasses conversation has two audio directions: the device hearing you and you hearing its answer. Turning up the speakers addresses only the second. In an [early Ray-Ban Meta owner discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/RayBanStories/comments/17cjb6q/possible_fatal_flaw_in_new_meta_raybans/), the original complaint was very faint or missing outgoing call audio. Later replies describe a microphone adjustment setting helping some people. The subreddit name does not make the product Ray-Ban Stories, and this historical report does not establish a current fault across generations.

The original owner's explanation of microphone placement is not a teardown diagnosis. Exact firmware, environmental noise and the outcome on every affected pair are not established by the thread.

## Describe what fails

Separate four observations: no wake acknowledgement, acknowledged command with wrong words, failed online response, and a reply too quiet to hear. An ordinary phone call with a consenting recipient can also reveal whether the issue concerns outgoing speech more broadly. Do not record other people without their agreement just to gather a sample.

Start in a quiet, safe location and use the normal supported fit. Compare that ordinary result with the environment where the problem occurs. Do not raise your voice repeatedly beside traffic or compromise awareness to hear a response.

## Use model-specific troubleshooting

[Meta's microphone troubleshooting guidance](https://www.meta.com/help/smart-glasses/articles/ray-ban-meta/troubleshoot-microphone-issues-ray-ban-meta-smart-glasses/) is the manufacturer reference. Follow instructions available for your model and app, including any supported adjustment. Do not copy an old menu path as if it must still exist, insert objects into microphone openings or modify the frame to imitate a commenter's proposed fix.

A setting that helps one wearer does not prove that fit caused another person's problem. If basic checks do not help, provide support with the exact activity, phone, app and firmware context. Keep account information and recordings private.

## Do not confuse listening features with command recognition

Features intended to help a wearer hear surrounding conversation have a different purpose from recognising a wake phrase or transmitting call audio. Their availability is not evidence that every microphone issue is solved. Likewise, a successful quiet-room command does not certify performance in a crowded venue.

The useful buying question is whether the supported voice interaction works in your ordinary setting, with a fallback when it does not. Reviewed November 3, 2026. Lensden did not test microphones, quantify noise rejection or establish a model-wide failure rate.
