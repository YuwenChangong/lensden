# Platform coverage and evidence boundaries

The available 2026-11-03 public searches returned first-person Reddit posts, direct XREAL Chinese community discussions, some Bilibili video descriptions, the Coolapk editorial hub, third-party Chinese hands-on reports and App Store user reviews. Those are distinct evidence types.

- **Reddit:** First-person individual reports and comments verified by exact post URL. Not prevalence statistics or independently reproduced defects.
- **Bilibili:** Descriptions for three actual 2026 user-experience videos verified; video transcripts and comment-level evidence not independently extracted. Do not attribute specific complaint to Bilibili commenters without accessing them.
- **Coolapk:** Editorial RayNeo GT showcase page accessible; individual first-person user posts in Coolapk were not reliably retrieved this session. Do not claim those posts were verified.
- **Baidu Tieba:** Exact direct posts were not reliably retrievable with searches this session. Do not invent them or cite search snippets as first-person testimony.
- **YouTube:** Direct relevant review/comment transcripts were not verified in this research pass. Future sourcing must obtain exact video URL, date, reviewed model, description/transcript context and separate commenter claims.
- **XREAL community:** Direct Chinese user posts (model and date) accessed, good primary complaints, but not independent factory defect validation.
- **Professional reviews/App Store:** Separate testing and app version observations from community reports; quote sparingly and link to originals.

Research is a meaningful public sample, **not exhaustive coverage of the internet**. Google, Bing and AI search results cannot be guaranteed by site publishing, sitemap submission or promotion.
