## Which resolution figure should a comparison use?

First establish what the manufacturer counted. A total for a two-display system is not the same quantity as the pixel dimensions of one eye's display. Apple's Vision Pro introduction described 23 million pixels across two displays. Its current M5-generation technical specification lists a 23-million-pixel micro-OLED display system. Neither statement should be silently relabeled as a per-eye width-by-height specification. [Apple introduction](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/06/introducing-apple-vision-pro/), [current technical specification](https://www.apple.com/apple-vision-pro/specs/)

## Build an evidence table before choosing a winner

| Field | Definition to preserve |
|---|---|
| Resolution | Per-eye dimensions, binocular total or another explicitly named quantity |
| Display technology | The manufacturer's exact term, with the model and generation |
| Field of view | Measurement direction and conditions, if disclosed |
| Brightness | Measurement location, unit and stated conditions |
| Refresh rate | Supported mode and any documented restrictions |
| Evidence | Primary source, access date and unresolved caveats |

This table is a proposed research method. It does not supply missing specifications. Use “not disclosed” when the source lacks a definition, and keep incompatible measurement contexts in separate columns. Do not manufacture a conversion merely to make a chart look complete.

## What this example can and cannot support

The Apple sources support an attributed display-system description. They do not establish Lensden's independent measurement of perceived clarity, comfort, optical quality or suitability for a particular person. The 2023 announcement and today's product specification have different dates and product context; check the exact generation before combining any other fields from them.

For a research report, state your model population and collection date before computing summaries. Missing fields should remain missing in the denominator discussion. A manufacturer's published number should retain its attribution, even when the presentation is concise. Those are editorial safeguards, not evidence that one device performs better than another.

## Limits of this comparison

Check model identity, definitions and primary-source links before combining figures. Add a direct comparison only when both sides have compatible evidence. This article contains no ranking, price, offer, user review, rating or hands-on testing claim.
